    No offline save found
    Start a new game to create one on this device.

    Desktop — W A S D or Arrow keys to walk. E to inspect or interact. B opens the handbook.

    Mobile — hold the joystick (lower-left) and tap the Interact button (lower-right).

    Chair — press E at a chair to sit with an agent; Space stands you up again.

    Read the CCS Handbook on the shelves at any time — all four quarters, with call scripts, email templates and metric formulas. Every question on the floor is answered inside it.

    Walk to a chair and press E to sit with an agent, then E again to work the computer. Space stands you up.

    Four terminals, two questions each (Q1–Q4). Collect the three review cards, then open the exit door and step through.

    Optional — the Mock Call Bay door in the top wall leads to a practice room with six extra questions and a bonus card.

    Room 2 — clear the training floor and the QA & Escalation Wing door opens on the right wall: six new console questions, three relics and the QA Wing handbook chapter.

    Robi — your robot companion hovers behind you with hints. Correct answers and review cards earn XP, coins, answer streaks and achievements (8 to find). Level up, then check your tally in the pause menu or at the end of the run.

    Progress is saved offline on this device — no account, no internet needed.
